- **Laser:** 5V KY-008 650nm laser transmitter modules
- **Additional Component:** B09LSFDFCT
- **Connectivity:** USB-C cable
- **Optional Accessories:** B0FXL6DLB9 and B0CL4KLL5C

> **Note:** The optional components are not necessary for the basic pan/tilt laser project. They are included as recommended accessories that may make construction and experimentation easier.
